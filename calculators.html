How to read these results

All money calculators assume interest compounds monthly unless noted. Loan and mortgage payments use the standard amortization formula P·r / (1 − (1+r)−n), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of payments. Projections such as compound growth, retirement and MRR are illustrations, not guarantees — real returns vary year to year.

Business tools use common definitions: gross margin is profit ÷ price, markup is profit ÷ cost, LTV is monthly gross profit per customer ÷ churn, and break-even is fixed costs ÷ contribution margin. AI cost tools ask you to enter current provider prices because model pricing changes frequently.
