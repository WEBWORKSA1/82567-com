Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

Calculator inputs never leave your device. We only receive information you choose to submit through a form. We use cookies for advertising (Google AdSense) and basic preferences (theme, currency, consent choice).

Information we collect

Forms: when you submit a contact, expert-help, donation, sponsorship, careers, contest or newsletter form, we receive the fields you fill in (such as name, email, phone, message). Forms are delivered to us by email through the FormSubmit service. Automatically: our host (GitHub Pages) and advertising partners may process standard log data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. Local storage: we save your theme, currency and cookie choice in your browser only.

How we use information

To answer your message; to match expert-help requests with a suitable partner (only after you consent on the form); to send a payment link for pledged donations; to administer contests; to send the newsletter you subscribed to; and to improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites. Choosing “Essential only” in our cookie banner requests non-personalized ads. You can reopen the banner from “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube using the privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie.com) player, which loads only when you click play. YouTube’s privacy policy then applies.

Sharing

We share form data only with service providers needed to operate the site (email delivery, hosting), with a partner when you request an introduction, or when required by law.

Retention and your rights

We keep messages only as long as needed for the purpose you sent them. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25 or CCPA/CPRA), you may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to processing. Send a request through our contact form choosing “Privacy request”.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
