About us

Math you can’t break down into excuses

82567.com is an independent website of free calculators and plain-English guides for personal finance, small business, AI budgeting and everyday numbers. The name comes from the number itself: 82,567 is prime — it can only be divided by 1 and itself. We think decisions about money and business deserve that kind of integrity.

What we believe

Show the formula. Every calculator uses standard, documented formulas, and our guides explain them. Respect privacy. Calculations run entirely in your browser; we don’t see what you type. Stay free. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships, partner introductions and voluntary donations — never by paywalls.

How calculators are checked

Each tool is tested against hand-worked examples and widely used reference formulas before publishing. When rules change — for example, AI model pricing — we ask you to enter current values instead of hard-coding numbers that go stale. If you find an error, tell us and we’ll fix it quickly.

How we make money

We display ads through Google AdSense, offer sponsorship placements, and may receive a referral fee when you choose to work with a partner through our expert-help service. These relationships never change a calculator’s output.
